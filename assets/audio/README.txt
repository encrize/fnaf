# Custom Audio Files
Place any custom .wav or .ogg sound effects here to replace the built-in procedural audio generator.
Supported names:
- door.wav
- light_buzz.wav
- footsteps.wav
- jumpscare.wav
- ambient.wav
- camera_switch.wav
- animatronic_move.wav
- fox_knock.wav
- power_out.wav
- kitchen.wav
- chime_6am.wav
